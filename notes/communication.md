# Communication & Approach

The real value is solution design and stakeholder communication — PRs are the easy part.
Optimize for value created and dollars saved, not output volume.

## What to get good at
- **Solution design** — frame the problem, options, trade-offs, cost; recommend one.
- **Reading the org** — understand dynamics in Slack threads and email, what's trending,
  and what the SLT (senior leadership team) cares most about this quarter.
- **Visibility** — know who to connect with; contribute where the work is visible and valued.
- **Cost framing** — express impact in $ saved / earned, latency, headcount-hours.
